English繁體中文

English

SURREALITY is an AR exhibition app by HKUST & HKUST(GZ) MC2 Lab. It has no account registration, chat or payment features. This policy explains app processing and the proposed voluntary evaluation.

Camera, location and recordings

ARKit processes person segmentation on your device. Cloud localization is separate: the XDK/Seengene service receives grayscale scene JPEGs, camera pose and calibration data, session/request information, device information and GPS data when available, to locate the AR exhibition. Scene images may include people nearby. Cloud-localization inputs are processed for positioning and are not saved.

Captured photos and videos are held locally; you can choose to save them to Photos or share them. Saving to Photos requires permission; sharing is handled by the service you choose. ReplayKit microphone recording is disabled.

Analytics and online content

Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, crash reporting and performance reporting are disabled from version 0.2.6, build 2026111105 onwards. Earlier builds may have used Unity Analytics. Any personal information already received by Unity remains subject to Unity’s privacy policy, including its retention criteria and applicable access, deletion and opt-out rights. You can contact us or Unity about such information.

The Info page loads surreality.world, Tencent COS media and OpenStreetMap maps. These requests disclose your IP address and request metadata to the relevant hosts. Their access-log practices are separate from cloud-localization non-retention; see the Tencent Cloud and OpenStreetMap policies.

Optional research evaluation

A voluntary evaluation may invite satisfaction ratings about the exhibition app and photo/video features. Participation requires choosing an initially unchecked option; declining does not affect app features. You can withdraw from future participation in Settings. Your participation choice is stored on the device. No rating submission service is currently enabled, and ratings are not collected automatically.

If ratings are collected, they will be anonymised, kept for three years and then deleted, and not linked to your name, email address or device identifier. They will be used to evaluate satisfaction with the stated features. Participation does not authorise uploading your photos, videos or identity information. Once ratings are irreversibly anonymised, we cannot identify and remove an individual’s ratings. The evaluation has received ethics approval from the team’s research institution.

Your choices and contact

You can manage camera, location and Photos permissions in iOS Settings; restricting permissions may limit related features. Deleting the app removes its local data; copies saved to Photos must be managed separately. The app’s cloud-localization configuration includes HTTP endpoints, so not all transmissions are encrypted.

For privacy or research questions, or requests to access, correct or delete personal information or withdraw consent where applicable, contact mc2@hkust-gz.edu.cn. For Unity-held information, you may also contact DPO@unity3d.com. We will update this policy when relevant practices change.

繁體中文

SURREALITY 是 HKUST & HKUST(GZ) MC2 Lab 開發的擴增實境展覽應用程式，不設帳戶註冊、聊天或付款功能。本政策說明應用程式的資料處理及擬提供的自願測評。

相機、定位與拍攝內容

ARKit 在你的裝置上處理人像分割。雲端定位是另一項處理：XDK/Seengene 服務會接收場景灰階 JPEG、相機位姿及校準資料、工作階段與請求資訊、裝置資訊，以及可取得的 GPS 資料，以定位 AR 展覽內容。場景影像可能包含附近的人。雲端定位輸入資料用於定位處理，不會保存。

拍攝的照片和影片保留於本機；你可主動選擇儲存至「照片」或分享。儲存至「照片」需要授權；分享內容由你選用的服務處理。ReplayKit 的麥克風錄音功能已關閉。

分析與網上內容

0.2.6 版本自 2026111105 包起已停用 Unity Analytics、Unity Ads、當機回報及效能回報。較早的包可能曾使用 Unity Analytics。Unity 已接收的個人資料仍受其私隱政策規管，包括保留準則及適用的查閱、刪除與退出權利。你可就這些資料聯絡我們或 Unity。

「資訊」頁會載入 surreality.world、騰訊 COS 媒體及 OpenStreetMap 地圖。請求會向相應主機披露你的 IP 位址及請求中繼資料。其存取紀錄處理與雲端定位資料不保存的安排分開；請參閱騰訊雲及 OpenStreetMap 的政策。

自願研究測評

自願測評可能邀請你就展覽應用程式及拍照／錄影功能提供滿意度評分。參與需要你主動勾選預設未勾選的選項；拒絕不影響應用程式功能。你可在「設定」退出後續參與。參與選擇保存在裝置上。目前尚未啟用評分提交服務，也不會自動收集評分。

如收集評分，資料會經匿名化處理、保留三年後刪除，並且不與你的姓名、電郵地址或裝置識別碼關聯。評分用於評估上述功能的滿意度。參與不代表授權上傳你的照片、影片或身分資料。評分一旦完成不可逆匿名化，我們便無法按個人身分找出及刪除其評分。這項測評已通過團隊所屬科研機構的倫理審查。

你的選擇與聯絡方式

你可在 iOS「設定」管理相機、定位及「照片」權限；限制權限可能影響相關功能。刪除應用程式會移除其本機資料；已存入「照片」的副本須另行管理。應用程式的雲端定位設定包含 HTTP 端點，因此並非所有傳輸均經加密。

如有私隱或研究問題，或希望在適用情況下查閱、更正、刪除個人資料或撤回同意，請聯絡 mc2@hkust-gz.edu.cn。涉及 Unity 持有的資料，也可聯絡 DPO@unity3d.com。相關做法改變時，我們會更新本政策。
